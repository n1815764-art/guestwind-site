Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Guestwind handles, why, where it is kept and what your choices are. It covers this website and the Guestwind service that hotels use.

Who is responsible

Guestwind is operated by Easybuy Technologies LLC. Its details are under Operator and contact below.

When a hotel uses Guestwind, the hotel is the controller of its guests' personal data: the hotel decides why and how that data is used. Easybuy Technologies LLC handles the data for the hotel as its processor, under a data processing agreement with the hotel.

For this website and for emails you send us, Easybuy Technologies LLC is the controller.

This website

This website has no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us

If you write to support@guestwind.com, we use your name, email address and message only to reply to you and to keep a record of the correspondence (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) or (f)). We delete the conversation when it is no longer needed. We do not sell or share it.

Hotel guests

When a hotel uses Guestwind, the service handles these kinds of data for that hotel:

  • messages that guests exchange with the hotel by email, in messaging apps and on the hotel's website;
  • the name, contact details and language of those guests;
  • enquiries, quotes and bookings;
  • whether a guest agreed to receive marketing messages;
  • the hotel's public reviews and the replies to them.

The hotel decides which channels and features are switched on. The data is used to answer guests, to send messages that guests agreed to, and to measure for the hotel what its marketing brought in. Where a guest gave the matching consent, the hotel can also use hashed contact details to build advertising audiences on its own Google or Meta account. We do not sell personal data.

Guests are told when they are talking to an AI assistant. Marketing emails go only to guests who confirmed that they want them, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

If you are a guest and want to use one of your rights, ask the hotel: it is the controller of your data. We help the hotel to answer.

Hotel staff

People at the hotel who use the Control Tower sign in with their work email address. We use it to give them access and to send sign-in emails and system emails, such as reports.

AI models

Guest messages are read by AI language models that draft replies for the hotel. The models run on Google Cloud's Vertex AI service in its EU multi-region. At present these are Google's Gemini models.

Where the data is kept

Guests' data is stored and processed in the European Union. Two tools for hotel staff work from the United States: system emails and approval alerts. They carry no guest content. The vendors we use, and where each of them works, are listed on the sub-processors page.

Access from the United States

Easybuy Technologies LLC is a company in the United States. Its staff can access hotel data from the United States to run and support the service. For this access we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, which form part of our data processing agreement with each hotel. We are not certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Connected Google, Meta and TikTok accounts

A person at the hotel who is authorised to manage the hotel's accounts can connect them to Guestwind. The consent screen is shown by Google, Meta or TikTok. This always applies:

  • The access is used only to provide the features the hotel switched on, for that hotel.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted, kept separate for each hotel, and never written to logs.
  • Data obtained through these connections is not sold and is not used for advertising to anyone else.
  • Guest data used for advertising audiences or for measuring conversions is sent to a platform only where the hotel has the guest's consent, and then only as hashed identifiers. Where a conversation takes place on a platform, that platform necessarily handles it.
  • The hotel's use of the connected accounts follows the platform's own rules.

Google

  • Google Ads: plan, build and report campaigns in the hotel's ad account. Campaigns are created paused. Customer Match lists use hashed identifiers only, and only for guests who consented.
  • Business Profile: read the hotel's listing and its reviews, draft replies for a person to approve, and apply listing fixes after approval.
  • YouTube: upload the hotel's own videos to its channel.

Guestwind's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Meta

  • The hotel's Facebook Page and Instagram account: publish the hotel's own posts, and read and answer messages.
  • WhatsApp Business: answer guest conversations. Guest phone numbers and message content necessarily go to Meta for this.
  • The ad account and catalogue: build campaigns (created paused) and keep the hotel's catalogue current.
  • Conversion events for guests who gave the matching consent, with hashed identifiers only.

TikTok

  • The hotel's TikTok ad account: build campaigns (created paused) and Spark Ads.
  • The hotel's catalogue.

TikTok receives no guest identifier from Guestwind: no hashed email address or phone number, and no click identifier tied to a guest.

Taking the access back

The hotel can remove Guestwind at any time in the settings of its Google, Meta or TikTok account, under connected or third-party apps.

Business contacts

A hotel can use Guestwind to write to business contacts such as travel advisors, event planners and tour operators. Every such message names the source of the address and carries a one-click opt-out. An opt-out is added to a list that applies across all hotels, so nobody who opted out is written to again.

How long data is kept

How long a hotel's data is kept is agreed in the data processing agreement with the hotel. When a hotel stops using Guestwind, its data is deleted or returned as that agreement says.

Your rights

You may ask for access to your personal data, for its correction or deletion, for its processing to be restricted, and for a copy in a portable form, and you may object to its processing. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

If you are a hotel guest, ask the hotel first. For this website and for emails you sent us, write to support@guestwind.com.

How to have your data deleted

  • If you are a hotel guest: ask the hotel, or write to support@guestwind.com and name the hotel. We pass your request to the hotel and carry out its instruction.
  • If you connected a Google, Meta or TikTok account for a hotel: remove Guestwind in that account's settings, and write to support@guestwind.com to have the stored data deleted.
  • If you emailed us: write to support@guestwind.com and we delete the conversation.
