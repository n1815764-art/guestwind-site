Terms of service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

These terms apply to hotels that use Guestwind. Guestwind is in pre-launch: we are working with our first hotels, and features are added over time. Each hotel also signs an order form and a data processing agreement with us.

The service

Guestwind is software that does sales and marketing work for a hotel. It answers guest enquiries, prepares the hotel's content and advertising, writes to guests who agreed to it and to business contacts, and measures what this work brought in. The hotel oversees the work in the Control Tower.

Approvals and autonomy

Most kinds of work start at "A person approves each item": a person at the hotel approves each item before it goes out. Three kinds of routine, bounded work start at "Acts and reports": bid and budget changes within a cap that a person approved, messages to guests who have consented, and follow-ups to businesses that have already been contacted.

These always stay with a person: a new budget, a raised cap, a contract, a discount, a creator's deal and the first message to a business contact.

A kind of work is given more freedom only when the hotel owner approves it, on a measured record and passing checks. No kind of work promotes itself. A kind of work loses a step of freedom automatically after any of these: spend that differs from what the advertising platform reports by more than half a percent, or by one unit of the currency if that is more; a measured result that is negative with confidence two weeks running; a message, a post or an ad that broke a rule and reached a guest or a platform; a platform's refusal on policy grounds; or a complaint about a message the AI wrote.

Accounts and authority

The hotel confirms that it holds, or is authorised to use, every platform account it connects, and accepts the platform terms that apply to automated work on those accounts. The hotel connects its accounts itself and can disconnect them at any time.

Budgets and spend

Advertising and creator spend is the hotel's money. Budgets and caps are agreed with the hotel and set by people, never by the software. A hotel that wants spending stopped tells Guestwind, which pauses it. The hotel can also stop its own work itself in the Control Tower, and only the owner lifts a stop.

Results

Results are measured against comparison groups, not promised. Guestwind does not guarantee any level of revenue.

Fees

The fees are set out in the order form signed with each hotel.

Data protection

The hotel is the controller of its guests' personal data. Guestwind handles that data as the hotel's processor under the data processing agreement. See the privacy policy and the sub-processors page.

Content and rights

The hotel keeps its rights in the footage, text and data it supplies, and gives Guestwind the right to use them to provide the service. The hotel supplies the releases for people who appear on camera.

Term, ending the service and liability

How long the service runs, how either side can end it, and each side's liability are agreed in writing with each hotel before the service starts.
