Sub-processors

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Guestwind is in pre-launch. This page lists the vendors the service is set up to use to process personal data for a hotel, and where each of them works. Each hotel's data processing agreement lists the vendors that are active for that hotel.

Vendors we use to run the service

VendorWhat it doesWhere the data is processed
Supabase The database that holds the service's data, sign-in for Control Tower users, and the encrypted store for a hotel's access tokens. EU, Frankfurt. Account, billing and support data can leave the region.
Hetzner Online The servers and file storage that run the service. EU, Germany or Finland.
Google Cloud (Vertex AI) The AI language models that read guest messages and draft replies, at present Google's Gemini models. Google Cloud's EU multi-region.
Amazon Web Services (Simple Email Service) Sends the hotel's emails to its guests. EU, Frankfurt (eu-central-1).
Resend Sign-in emails and system emails to hotel staff. Never guest email. Sent through its region in Ireland. Email logs and account data are stored in the United States.
Slack Approval requests and alerts for the hotel's team. These carry no guest content. United States.

Where a vendor processes personal data outside the EU, the transfer rests on Standard Contractual Clauses or on that vendor's own certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

The hotel's own accounts and systems

The hotel holds these accounts and the contracts for them. Guestwind connects to them when the hotel authorises it, and only for the features the hotel switched on.

  • Meta: the hotel's Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp Business number, ad account and catalogue.
  • Google: the hotel's Google Ads account, Business Profile and YouTube channel.
  • TikTok: the hotel's ad account and catalogue.
  • The hotel's property management system, booking engine and channel manager.

Optional features

Some optional features use further vendors, for example a phone assistant or AI-made video. Such a vendor is added to this page, and the hotel is told and can object, before it is used for that hotel.
